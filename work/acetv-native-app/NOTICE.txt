Ace TV 0.14.1 — TV-local P2P receiver
The Ace TV receiver additions and acetv-wire-wasm are provided under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
This application includes ace-wire from Outpace by jamezrin and contributors (AGPL-3.0-or-later), commit dba774d870e08d2778748196e145455c74f22152.
Corresponding source, Cargo.lock, modifications and build scripts are available in the AC TV public repository.
Outpace: https://github.com/jamezrin/outpace
Samsung WebAssembly SDK runtime is generated by the Samsung Emscripten toolchain. Samsung AVPlay is provided by TV firmware.
No user channel identifier or favorite list is bundled in this application.
